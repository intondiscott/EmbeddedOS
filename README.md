# EmbeddedOS is a custom-made OS by me to give an Android-like experience on your esp32-type device. 
**Boards supported at the moment are as follows:**
- Lilly-GO T-Deck
- M5Stack Core 2
- WaveShare OLED 2.06 Smartwatch

  :exclamation: 
  There may be broken parts at the moment due to testing. Please let me know of issues, and I will fix them accordingly.
